# Digital Marketing Assignment

Q1. Zomato / Swiggy ki 5 Digital Marketing Activities
1. Social Media Marketin 
Zomato aur Swiggy Instagram, Facebook aur other social media platforms par food-related posts, memes, reels aur offers share karte hain. Isse brand awareness badhti hai aur naye users ko app ke baare mein pata chalta hai.
2. Discounts and Offers
Zomato aur Swiggy new users ko attractive discounts, coupons aur free delivery jaise offers dete hain. Isse users ko app download karke first order karne ke liye motivation milti hai.
3. Influencer Marketing 
Zomato aur Swiggy food bloggers aur social media influencers ke saath collaboration karte hain. Influencers jab app ke offers ya restaurants ke baare mein batate hain, to unke followers bhi app ko try karne ke liye interested hote hain.
4. Push Notifications 
Apps users ko push notifications ke through special offers, discounts aur food recommendations bhejti hain. Isse users ko app dobara open karne aur order place karne ke liye encourage kiya jaata hai.
5. Google Ads and Search Marketing
Jab koi person Google par food delivery near. me ya order food online search karta hai. to Zomato ya Swiggy ke ads show ho sakte hain. Isse interested customers directly app ya website par aa sakte hain.

## Q3. Flipkart ke New Feature ko Promote Karne ka Plan

Agar Flipkart ek new feature launch karta hai, to main sabse pehle Instagram aur Facebook par us feature ki reels aur posts share karunga.

Main YouTube aur Instagram ke tech influencers ke saath collaboration karke feature ka demo karwaunga.

Existing users ko email aur push notifications ke through new feature ke baare mein inform karunga.

Users ko feature try karne ke liye special discount ya limited-time offer provide karunga.

YouTube par short videos banakar feature ke benefits aur use karne ka process explain karunga.

Finally, main user feedback aur engagement ko check karke marketing campaign ko improve karunga.


Q4. New Music Playlist Feature ke liye 5-Step Digital Marketing Plan

Step 1 – Social Media Promotion
Instagram, Facebook aur YouTube par new playlist feature ki teaser videos aur reels post karenge. Isse users ke andar curiosity create hogi.

Step 2 – Influencer Marketing 
Music influencers aur YouTubers ke saath collaboration karenge. Influencers apni favourite playlist create karke feature ko demonstrate karenge.

Step 3 – Email Marketing 
Existing users ko personalized emails bhejenge, jisme new playlist feature ke benefits aur use karne ka simple process bataya jayega.

Step 4 – Push Notifications  
Users ko app notifications bhejenge, jaise "Create Your Perfect Playlist Today!" Isse users ko feature try karne ke liye encourage kiya jayega.

Step 5 – Social Media Challenge  
My Perfect Playlist Challenge" start karenge. Users apni favourite playlist create karke social media par share karenge. Best playlist create karne wale users ko rewards ya premium subscription diya ja sakta hai.

 Conclusion

Is digital marketing campaign ka main goal users ko new feature ke baare mein aware karna, unki interest create karna aur unhe feature try karne ke liye encourage karna hoga. Isse app ki engagement aur active users increase ho sakte hain.
