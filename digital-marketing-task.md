Q2. One-Page Marketing Plan for a New App Feature

App Selected: Swiggy**

New Feature: Group Order

 1. Marketing Objective
Swiggy ke Group Order feature ke baare mein awareness badhana aur zyada se zyada users ko is feature ka use karne ke liye encourage karna.

2. Target Audience
- College students jo apne friends ke saath food order karte hain.
- Working professionals jo office mein colleagues ke saath lunch order karte hain.
- Families aur friends jo ek saath different food items order karna chahte hain.

 3. Key Message
"Order Together, Enjoy Together! Swiggy ke Group Order feature se friends aur family ke saath food order karna hua easy."

 4. Marketing Channels

- Instagram Marketing:** Reels aur posts ke through dikhayenge ki friends ek saath food order kaise kar sakte hain.
  YouTube Marketing:** Short videos banayenge jo Group Order feature ka use karna samjhayengi.
- Email Marketing:** Existing users ko emails bhejkar new feature aur uske benefits ke baare mein batayenge.
- Push Notifications:** App users ko notifications bhejkar feature try karne ke liye encourage karenge.
- Influencer Marketing:** Food influencers se feature promote karwayenge, taaki unke followers bhi ise try karein.

 5. Basic Marketing Timeline

| Week | Marketing Activity |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Promotional posts, reels aur videos prepare karna. |
| Week 2 | Instagram aur YouTube par campaign launch karna. |
| Week 3 | Influencers, emails aur push notifications ke through promotion karna. |
| Week 4 | Campaign ke results aur feature usage ko check karna. |

6. Success Measurement
Campaign ki success ko completed group orders, feature usage, social media engagement aur customer feedback ke through measure kiya jaayega.

Q3. Five Digital Marketing Channels for a New Music Playlist Feature
Objective:** Ek music streaming app mein new collaborative playlist feature ko promote karna, jisse users apne friends ke saath playlists create kar sakein.

 1. Instagram Marketing:** Instagram Reels aur Stories ke through young music lovers tak easily reach kiya ja sakta hai.

2. YouTube Marketing:** YouTube videos aur Shorts ke through new playlist feature ka demo dikhaya ja sakta hai.

3. Influencer Marketing:** Music influencers apne followers ko feature ke baare mein bata sakte hain aur unhe playlists create karne ke liye encourage kar sakte hain.

4. Email Marketing:** Existing users ko emails bhejkar new feature ke benefits aur use karne ka tarika samjhaya ja sakta hai.

5. Push Notifications:** App notifications ke through users ko new feature try karne aur apne friends ko playlist mein invite karne ke liye remind kiya ja sakta hai.

Q4. Three Measurable KPIs for an IPL Refer & Earn Campaign

Campaign:** IPL Season ke dauran Cricket Streaming App ke liye Refer & Earn Campaign.

1. Number of New Users:** Campaign ke through kitne naye users app par register karte hain, ise track karenge. Isse pata chalega ki campaign kitne naye customers la raha hai.

2. Referral Code Usage:** Kitne naye users referral codes ka successfully use karte hain, ise measure karenge. Isse pata chalega ki users apne friends ko kitna refer kar rahe hain.

3. App Downloads:** Campaign ke dauran kitne app downloads milte hain, unhe track karenge. Isse campaign ki reach aur naye users attract karne ki ability samajh aayegi.

Conclusion:** In 3 KPIs ki help se company samajh sakti hai ki IPL ke dauran Refer & Earn campaign kitna successful raha aur usse kitne naye users mile.
